## 🏛️ 1. Executive Summary

India is home to over **7 million traditional artisans and craftspersons**, over 74% of whom belong to Scheduled Castes (SC), Other Backward Classes (OBC), De-Notified & Nomadic Tribes, and marginalized rural women. Despite world-renowned mastercraft heritage:
- **70–85% of retail product value** is absorbed by exploitative intermediaries and aggregators.
- **Low digital literacy & linguistic barriers** prevent direct onboarding onto formal e-commerce platforms (Amazon, Flipkart, GeM).
- **Inability to fulfill bulk institutional orders** due to fragmented, single-artisan workshop capacities.
- **Counterfeiting and unverified knockoffs** erode consumer trust in authentic Geographical Indication (GI) handcrafts.
- **Lack of formal credit history** traps artisans in predatory local money-lending cycles ($36\text{–}60\%\text{ APR}$).

**Karighar** directly solves PS #26090 by delivering a full-stack, voice-native, AI-augmented mobile operating system and marketplace that elevates marginalized artisans into self-sovereign global exporters and institutional suppliers.

### Pillar 1: Voice-First Dialect Inclusivity ("Setu Didi")
- **Problem**: 68% of marginalized rural craftspeople cannot type or navigate complex English app forms.
- **Innovation**: Real-time integration of Digital India Bhashini Speech-to-Listing pipelines. Artisans speak naturally in **Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, or Indian English**.
- **Result**: Automated extraction of craft title, raw materials, dimensions, and craftsmanship story into dual-language SEO listings.

### Pillar 2: Edge-AI Computer Vision & Studio Matrix
- **Problem**: High photography fees (₹500–₹1,500/product) prevent artisans from creating attractive listings.
- **Innovation**: Real-time 4K Neural studio filters (Warm Sunlight, Studio Softbox, Heritage Silk Glow) with background elimination.
- **Weave Inspector**: Edge computer vision algorithm analyzes warp and weft thread density ($120\text{ EPI} \times 110\text{ PPI}$), auto-certifying Grade A+ handloom authenticity.

### Pillar 3: Cryptographic GI Craft Passport
- **Problem**: Powerloom synthetic sarees and plastic pottery are masqueraded as genuine GI handicrafts.
- **Innovation**: Every item is minted with a unique SHA-256 cryptographic provenance hash, embedding Geo-coordinates, Master Artisan UID, and Weaver Service Centre batch number into a consumer-scannable QR code.

### Pillar 4: Institutional B2B Tenders & Cluster Pooling
- **Problem**: Government departments, embassies, and hotels issue 500+ unit corporate gifting tenders that single artisans cannot supply alone.
- **Innovation**: Autonomous Order Pooling engine that aggregates fragmented village clusters (e.g., 10 Varanasi weavers contributing 50 sarees each to win a ₹12,50,000 Ministry tender), with automated GeM/GST invoicing (`HSN 5007`).

### Pillar 5: Varta-AI Wage-Defense Sentry & Global Export
- **Problem**: Corporate buyers exploit rural craftspeople through aggressive lowball negotiations.
- **Innovation**: **Varta-AI** acts as an autonomous defense sentry, computing minimum viable artisan day-wages ($MVAR \ge ₹850/\text{day}$) and countering opportunistic bids automatically.
- **Export Gateway**: 1-tap conversion of domestic listings into international export orders in USD (\$), EUR (€), GBP (£), and AED (د.إ) with automated DGFT Certificate of Origin compliance.

### Pillar 6: PM-Vishwakarma "Karighar Credit" & PFMS Escrow
- **Problem**: Working capital starvation and payment defaults by distant buyers.
- **Innovation**: Milestone-based Smart Delivery Escrow holding buyer payments until verified delivery, triggering instant settlement directly into artisan bank accounts via PFMS / UPI.
- **Alternative Underwriting**: Computes a holistic **Karighar Credit Score (300–900)** based on craft verification, customer satisfaction, and on-time fulfillment, qualifying artisans for 1-tap ₹1,00,000 collateral-free micro-loans at 5% subsidized interest under PM-Vishwakarma.

## 🛠️ 5. Technology Architecture

- **Mobile & Web Frontend**: Flutter 3.47.3 (Dart 3.7.0), BLoC 9.x state architecture, Clean Repository Pattern, GoRouter declarative navigation.
- **Design Tokens**: Accessible Indic cultural palette (Deep Saffron `#E8833A`, Trust Teal `#1A6B6A`, Heritage Charcoal `#2D3142`), minimum $48\text{dp}$ touch surfaces, WCAG AAA contrast.
- **Synchronous Real-Time Engine**: Built-in Server-Sent Events (SSE) synchronization broker (`serve_flutter.js`) enabling instantaneous cross-device status sync between Buyer, Artisan, and Admin dashboards without external third-party dependencies.
- **Immersive AR Viewer**: WebXR / Canvas 3D rendering simulation with interactive ambient lighting controls (`/buyer/ar/:id`).
- **Telemetry GIS**: National MoSJE cluster health radar plotting artisan density, active orders, and distress alerts on dynamic canvas maps (`/admin/gis-map`).
